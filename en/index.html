Brandbook

The rules of my personal brand: black, white, a thin line and plenty of empty space.

Mark

sizes: 16, 24 and 40 px

A circle with a dot in the centre, 1 px stroke. Clear space equals the circle radius. Ink on light backgrounds, paper on dark - always one colour.

Colours

  • Paper - background #FAFAF7
  • Ink - text, lines, mark #111111 · contrast on paper 18.1:1
  • Grey - secondary text #6E6E6A · contrast on paper 4.9:1
  • Grey - metadata #73736E · contrast on paper 4.6:1
  • Line - 1 px dividers rgba(17, 17, 17, .14)
  • Black - dark mode background #000000

Typography

Helvetica Neue - two weights: 400 and 500. No all caps, no bold inside a sentence.

  • title 20 / 18 px, 500teraz · loom.studio · reh.ab
  • body 15 px, 400, line height 1.6I design identities, websites and digital products - from idea to launch. I run loom.studio.
  • metadata 12 px, 40001 · 02 · 03 · marketplace · studio · pitch deck

Symbols and icons

Every project has its own geometric symbol drawn with a 1 px stroke, no fills. Interface icons follow the same style.

Grid

12 columns, max 1280 px, 6vw margin (24 px on phones), 24 px gutter, 8 px vertical rhythm.

Motion

  • drawing the mark1.8 s
  • text fade-in600 ms, 80 ms apart
  • row underline400 ms
  • project auto-advance3 s (with animation: 8 s)
  • sheet slide-up700 ms
  • reduced motion in the systemeverything stops

Slow and quiet motion: cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1), no springs, bounces or parallax.

Rules

Yes

  • plenty of empty space
  • dividers only as 1 px lines
  • one colour: ink or paper
  • small type sizes, two weights

No

  • accent colours in my brand
  • shadows, gradients and rounded corners
  • all caps and bold inside a sentence
  • decorative icons and emoji

Colours and gradients belong only to project brands (e.g. teraz) - inside their frames. The one exception: the rainbow glow of the AI assistant.
